Case study: Food Rush · from player friction to retention

Problem

Players weren't forming long chains, which capped satisfaction and limited session length. Observed through play behaviour, not guesswork.

Solution

Reduced interaction friction with diagonal linking, and added slow motion during drag so every move felt controlled and rewarding.

Result

Higher engagement and longer sessions, reaching 15% D1 retention and 8m+ average session.
